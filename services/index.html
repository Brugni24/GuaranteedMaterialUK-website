ROOF CONDITIONING SURVEYS

Roof Conditioning Surveys

Our Roofing Survey process consists of:

On-site assessment: through our network of Technical Advisors we can make a full condition review with drone surveys, intrusive leak investigations, ELD & Steam testing, core sampling, CWCT Testing and moisture mapping all carried out with minimal disruption.
Detailed report: you will receive a lifecycle and PPM plans, cost consulting, design specifications and tendering services.
