Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains how Guaranteed Material UK collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us provide accurate estimates and complete your construction project. This includes information you share with us directly and information gathered automatically when you use our website.

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and property address.
  • Project details, such as the type of service requested, site measurements, photos, and scheduling preferences.
  • Usage data collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring links.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to deliver professional roofing systems, technical consultations, and reliable customer service. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Provide estimates and assessments: Prepare technical specifications, site surveys, and proposals for your flat roofing requirements.
  • Fulfill orders and supply: Coordinate component deliveries, on-site logistics, and official handover documentation.
  • Issue and maintain warranties: Administer our 10 to 35-year comprehensive system guarantees and ongoing aftercare.
  • Communicate with you: Respond to enquiries, schedule site visits, and provide ongoing technical assistance.
  • Improve our website and services: Monitor usage trends and website performance to optimize client experience.
  • Ensure regulatory compliance: Comply with legal, accounting, tax, and building standards under UK law.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing required to fulfill an agreement or take requested steps prior to entering a contract.
  • Legitimate Interests: Necessary for managing our commercial operations, security, and quality standards, without overriding your rights.
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with statutory UK tax, accounting, and construction regulations.
  • Consent: Where explicitly provided for optional communications or non-essential cookies.

How We Share Your Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share details with trusted third parties strictly necessary to deliver our services:

  • Logistics and Haulage Partners: To dispatch roofing membranes, insulation, and accessories directly to your designated site.
  • Approved Technical Surveyors: Certified professionals who perform site inspections and warranty verification.
  • Professional Advisers: Trusted IT, legal, insurance, and accounting providers supporting our legitimate operations.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Solely where mandated by applicable law or official regulatory requests.

Data Security & Retention

We maintain robust technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Because Guaranteed Material UK issues long-term building guarantees ranging from 10 to 35 years, technical project records, site survey documents, and warranty contracts are retained for the duration of the guarantee period to ensure valid claim administration. Routine client correspondence is reviewed and securely deleted periodically.

Your Legal Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability regarding your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at office@gmukltd.co.uk. We respond to all verified requests within one calendar month.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies for core browsing security and optional analytics cookies to measure page performance. You may configure or block cookies at any time via your browser settings.
