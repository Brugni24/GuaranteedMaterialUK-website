RBM SYSTEMS

Our Systems

Our Bituminous Membrane Systems provide versatile, high-performance waterproofing solutions for every roofing application. Combining advanced membrane technologies — including APAO, SBS and Self-Adhesive — with a variety of build-up configurations, we deliver tailored levels of performance, durability, and protection, backed by solid warranties.

FULL APAO COMPOUND - NO AGEING

PHOENIX SUPER 35

Discover

Phoenix Super membranes feature the APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound, combining the high-temperature and UV resistance of APP compounds with the low-temperature flexibility and elasticity of SBS compounds.

GUARANTEE 35 Years
COMPOUND APAO

SBS COMPOUND - HIGH-END

SATURN 30

Discover

Saturn membranes use a special SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) modified bitumen compound, the leading waterproofing solution for flat and low-slope roofs across the UK. With its cold flexibility (-30°C) and rubber-like, elastic behaviour, it represents the high-end SBS proposal to offer incredible performances and durability.

GUARANTEE 30 Years
COMPOUND SBS

APAO AND SA COMPOUNDS - HYBRID

PHOENIX SUPER 25

Discover

This hybrid system combines a Phoenix Super cap sheet membrane made of APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound with Halley Tech underlay made of a self-adhesive compound modified with special adhesive and resins mixture that allows for excellent flame-free application.

GUARANTEE 25 Years
COMPOUND APAO & SA

SBS COMPOUND - ROBUST SYSTEM

MERCURY 20

Discover

Mercury membranes feature the SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) modified compound, with good characteristics allowing them to stand alongside the best SBS membranes on the market.

GUARANTEE 20 Years
COMPOUND SBS

SA COMPOUND - FLAME FREE

HALLEY 20

Discover

Halley membranes are made of a self-adhesive compound modified with special adhesive and resins mixture that allows for excellent flame-free application: therefore safe and free from CO2 emissions.
