RBM SYSTEMS

Our Systems

Our Bituminous Membrane Systems provide versatile, high-performance waterproofing solutions for every roofing application. Combining advanced membrane technologies — including APAO, SBS and Self-Adhesive — with a variety of build-up configurations, we deliver tailored levels of performance, durability, and protection, backed by solid warranties.

FULL APAO COMPOUND - NO AGEING

PHOENIX SUPER 35

Discover

Phoenix Super membranes feature the APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound, combining the high-temperature and UV resistance of APP compounds with the low-temperature flexibility and elasticity of SBS compounds.

GUARANTEE 35 Years
COMPOUND APAO

SBS COMPOUND - HIGH-END

SATURN 30

Discover

Saturn membranes use a special SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) modified bitumen compound, the leading waterproofing solution for flat and low-slope roofs across the UK. With its cold flexibility (-30Â°C) and rubber-like, elastic behaviour, it represents the high-end SBS proposal to offer incredible performances and durability.

PHOENIX SUPER 25

Discover

This hybrid system combines a Phoenix Super cap sheet membrane made of APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound with Halley Tech underlay made of a self-adhesive compound modified with special adhesive and resins mixture that allows for excellent flame-free application.

GUARANTEES

Our Guarantee Approach

Too often, when it comes to roofing systems and guarantees, the focus falls on product performance alone.

We take a different approach — offering a durable, sustainable, and cost-efficient solution that starts with a professional, experienced Roof Survey. This survey then informs a precise Design Specification, ensuring the most Appropriate Roofing System is selected for each project.

A carefully selected network of Approved Installers guarantees Correct Installation, supported by our constant On-site Inspection to meet the highest standards.

Through this process, we're able to issue a Guarantee based on the system as installed — one that stays valid only through ongoing Roof Maintenance, carried out by the Approved Installer according to our specifications.
