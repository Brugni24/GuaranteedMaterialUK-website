INTRODUCTION

Phoenix Super 25

This hybrid system is made of a Phoenix Super cap sheet — an APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound that combines the high-temperature and UV resistance of APP with the low-temperature flexibility and elasticity of SBS — and a Halley Tech underlay, formulated with a self-adhesive compound of special adhesives and resins for excellent adhesion and flame-free application.

KEY PROPERTIES

Why use APAO & SA membranes

Climate suitability is a primary driver to choose the APAO capsheet. The UK's temperature range — cold, damp winters and increasingly warm summers — makes this unique compound particularly well suited, as it prevents cold-weather cracking and resists thermal cycling stress in the UK's frequent freeze-thaw conditions. The SA UL eliminates the open flame risk factor on site during installation and allows direct installation on sensitive substrates, where heat exposure would otherwise pose a risk.

Superior durability of the APAO capsheet compared to traditional polymer-modified bitumen membranes, with an estimated service life by BBA in excess of 40 years.

Good adhesion of the SA underlay membrane to the most common substrates (concrete, wood, metal) and no-risk when installed on heat sensitive substrates.

Resistance to ageing (UV and thermal stress) thanks to the stable APAO polymeric structure.
