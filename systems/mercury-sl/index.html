INTRODUCTION

Mercury SL

Mercury membranes use an SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) modified bitumen compound, the most common waterproofing solution for roofs across the UK.

With its cold flexibility (-20°C) and elastic behaviour, Mercury SL represents a good balance with quality and performances in a single layer system.

KEY PROPERTIES

Why use SBS membranes

SBS systems have decades of proven performance data in the UK market because they are particularly well-suited to the local weather, with good elasticity and adhesion — especially when compared to APP systems.

Superior elasticity and flexibility compared to traditional APP polymer-modified bitumen membranes.

Good adhesion to the most common substrates (concrete, wood, metal).
