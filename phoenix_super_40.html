Phoenix Super 40

INTRODUCTION

Phoenix Super 40

Phoenix Super membranes feature the APAO (amorphous polyalphaolefin) modified compound, combining the high-temperature and UV resistance of APP compounds with the low-temperature flexibility and elasticity of SBS compounds.

40 Years Warranty BBA Certified Torch-Applied
2
KEY PROPERTIES

Why use APAO membranes

Climate suitability is a primary driver. The UK's temperature range — cold, damp winters and increasingly warm summers — makes APAO particularly well suited, as it prevents cold-weather cracking and resists thermal cycling stress in the UK's frequent freeze-thaw conditions.

Superior durability compared to traditional polymer-modified bitumen membranes, with an estimated service life by BBA in excess of 40 years.

Good adhesion to the most common substrates (concrete, wood, metal).

Resistance to ageing (UV, oxidation) thanks to the stable polymeric structure.
