Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Guaranteed Material UK uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They are widely used by website operators to make websites work efficiently, recognize your device across sessions, and provide analytical information about how visitors navigate web content.

Cookies may be session cookies (which are deleted automatically when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (which remain stored on your device until they expire or you delete them manually).

How We Use Cookies

Guaranteed Material UK Ltd uses cookies to ensure our website functions correctly, to maintain system security, and to gain insight into how our technical product sheets, roofing systems, and service descriptions are accessed by users.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information without your explicit knowledge, nor do we sell or disclose cookie data to third-party advertisers.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Our website uses the following categories of cookies in compliance with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for you to browse the website and use its core features, such as page navigation, secure header states, and form submissions. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous information about how visitors use our site (e.g. which roofing system pages are visited most often, page loading speeds, and referral sources). This helps us improve the user journey and optimize site responsiveness.
  • Functionality Cookies: These cookies enable the website to remember choices you make (such as preferences or language settings) to provide enhanced and more personalized features.

Third-Party Technologies

In some instances, we may use third-party services that set cookies on our behalf. For example:

  • Web Analytics: To help us measure web traffic and compile anonymous site performance statistics.
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) & Fonts: To load fonts (such as Google Fonts) and script libraries securely and efficiently across global server nodes.

These third parties are subject to their own privacy policies and contractual standards regarding data protection.

Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies at any time through your internet browser settings. Most browsers allow you to decline all cookies, accept only certain cookies, or alert you every time a cookie is sent to your device.

To manage cookies on common web browsers, please refer to the official support documentation:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies

Please note that if you choose to block strictly necessary cookies, some sections or functionalities of our website may not operate as intended.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory adjustments. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
