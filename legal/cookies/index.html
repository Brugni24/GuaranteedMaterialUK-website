Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Guaranteed Material UK uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They are widely used by website operators to make websites work efficiently, recognize your device across sessions, and provide analytical information about how visitors navigate web content.

Cookies may be session cookies (which are deleted automatically when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (which remain stored on your device until they expire or you delete them manually).

How We Use Cookies

Guaranteed Material UK Ltd uses cookies to ensure our website functions correctly, to maintain system security, and to gain insight into how our technical product sheets, roofing systems, and service descriptions are accessed by users.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information without your explicit knowledge, nor do we sell or disclose cookie data to third-party advertisers.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Our website uses the following categories of cookies in strict compliance with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for you to browse the website, ensure network security, enable core navigation features, and store your cookie consent preferences. Under PECR and UK GDPR, these cookies do not require prior consent because the website cannot function properly without them.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Optional cookies that collect aggregated, pseudonymous information about how visitors interact with our website (such as page visit frequency, popular roofing system specifications, session duration, and referral sources). These cookies are only activated if you provide explicit prior consent.

Google Analytics 4 & Third-Party Technologies

Subject to your opt-in consent, our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analysis service provided by Google Ireland Limited (for users located in the UK and European Economic Area) and Google LLC (United States).

Technical Architecture & Basic Consent Mode: We implement Google Consent Mode v2 in Basic mode. This means no Google Analytics script is loaded, and no measurement hits or cookie requests are sent to Google servers prior to your explicit consent.

IP Processing in GA4: In Google Analytics 4, IP addresses are not logged, stored, or made accessible to the site owner. IP addresses are used solely at the edge collection servers to derive coarse geographic location data (country, city) and are immediately discarded.

Client Lifespan vs. Server-Side Data Retention: A distinction exists between the duration of cookies stored in your browser (up to 2 years by default, unless deleted sooner by your browser settings) and the retention of event-level and user-level data stored on Google Analytics servers. Server-side event retention can be configured in the Google Analytics admin panel between 2 months and 14 months; the exact operational retention period is determined by Guaranteed Material UK Ltd's administration settings.

International Transfers: Where data is processed outside the UK or EEA, Google relies on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and UK Extension).

Managing and Withdrawing Cookie Consent

You have full control over your cookie choices. You can modify or withdraw your consent for optional analytics cookies at any time directly on this website:

You can also control or delete cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to decline all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or receive an alert when a cookie is placed on your device:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Apple Safari: Settings / Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies

Please note that strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled through our consent manager as they are indispensable for secure navigation and site operation.
